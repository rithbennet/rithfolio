About

Hi, I'm Harith.

Updated Oct 7, 2026

My first computer was my dad's old laptop with 500 MB of RAM.

I'm a tech nerd from Kuala Lumpur who likes the small, gritty details of how things work. Right now I'm building DagangNow, an agentic commerce platform for Malaysian sellers.

It started when I was fifteen. My parents were strict, so no phone, but my dad kept a lot of old gear, including a laptop with 500 MB of RAM. I wanted the internet badly enough to borrow my cousin's CD drive, burn Linux distros onto CDs and keep installing until one worked. After that I was opening up compact HP office PCs to squeeze graphics cards into them, and later spending whatever I saved on microphones and audio gear.

At uni I took the tech foundation instead of choosing between electrical and software engineering, then picked software because I wanted to work remotely. In my second year I became the camera guy: I learned from my mentor Fika Azman, started the Sakura Sakura Club, and helped a lecturer build a studio from nothing. In third year I swapped the camera for hackathons, lost most of them, and learned Next.js along the way.

My internship at Asterisk Digital put me on a React Native team, then on AWS infrastructure, and then on data engineering for a gas company, where I helped prototype an AI tool for a whole department. Now I'm building my own thing.
