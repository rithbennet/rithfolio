# Shot list: photo cut-outs

Each object on the site is a photo of the real thing, cut out with a transparent background. The site adds the paper-cut edge and the shadow in CSS, so don't bake either into the photo. That keeps every object consistent, and the edge can still be switched on or off.

## How to shoot them

You built a studio, so this is your home turf:

1. **Light:** one large soft source at about 45° from the top-left, the same for every object, so they look like they're lying on one desk. Add a white bounce on the right.
2. **Background:** plain and matte, in a colour that contrasts with the object: white for dark objects, mid-grey for light ones. No props touching the object.
3. **Camera:** tripod, f/8 to f/11, ISO 100, 50 to 90 mm to avoid wide-angle distortion. Shoot straight down for flat things (CD, badge, sticker, print) and at a low three-quarter angle for objects (camera, mic, GPU).
4. **Export:** RAW, then 3000 px on the long edge. Cut out with Photoshop's Select Subject, Lightroom masking or Lift Subject on macOS. Save as PNG with transparency. Keep the soft contact shadow off; the site draws it.
5. **Naming:** `object-name.png` (e.g. `sony-a7r3.png`, `gpu.png`). Drop them in one folder and I'll wire them in.
